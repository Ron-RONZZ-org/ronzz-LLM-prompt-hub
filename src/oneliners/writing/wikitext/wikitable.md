## Task

Convert/represent information in a simple wikitable

## Requirements

- class: wikitable
- use Katex for math ($...$ delimiter, applies to any number/symbols)
- No extra formatting

Example:

```wikitext
{| class="wikitable"
|+Quarks and Antiquarks
|-
! Name !! Symbol !! Antiparticle !! Spin !! Charge !! B !! S !! c !! b !! t !! Mass $(GeV/c^2)$
|-
| Up || $u$ || $\bar{u}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{2}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0.005$
|-
| Down || $d$ || $\bar{d}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0.008$
|-
| Strange || $s$ || $\bar{s}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $\pm1$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0.50$
|-
| Charmed || $c$ || $\bar{c}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{2}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $0$ || $\pm1$ || $0$ || $0$ || $1.6$
|-
| Bottom || $b$ || $\bar{b}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $0$ || $0$ || $\pm1$ || $0$ || $5$
|-
| Top || $t$ || $\bar{t}$ || $1/2$ || $\pm\frac{2}{3}e$ || $\pm\frac{1}{3}$ || $0$ || $0$ || $0$ || $\pm1$ || $173$
|}
```
