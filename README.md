# ronzz.ORG prompt library

Ready-made AI prompts for the ronzz.ORG team — for writing and proofreading,
research, wiki editing, and software development. Open a prompt, copy it, adapt
it to your task, and paste it into your AI assistant.

**Just want to use the prompts?** → **[User guide](USERGUIDE.md)**
**Want to add or improve a prompt?** → **[Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md)**

## What you get

- A **searchable online prompt store** — every prompt grouped by theme, with a
  one-click copy button.
- **Ready-to-use prompts** for common tasks: proofread this, fact-check and
  cite, rewrite in my style, draft a wiki page, and many more.
- **Specialised AI assistants** ("Models"), such as *Veramiko Science* for
  research and *RonzzWikiCowriter* for the ronzz wiki.
- The **shared configuration** behind the ronzz.ORG coding agents.

## Quick start

1. Open the **prompt store** or **[Open WebUI](https://llm.ronzz.org)**.
2. Search, or browse by theme / type `/` in a chat to list the prompts.
3. **Copy** the prompt, replace the `{{fill-in markers}}`, and paste it into
   your AI assistant.

That's it — no technical setup required. The [user guide](USERGUIDE.md) explains
each step in detail, including where the fill-in markers go and how to get the
best results.

The prompts are written once under `src/`, compiled into copy-paste-ready text
in `dist/`, and published automatically to Open WebUI and the prompt store.
